Qalb

Black to move

Black: 2  ·  White: 2

How to play

Click an empty square or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter to place a disc. A move is legal only if it captures at least one opposing disc by sandwiching a run of opponents between your new disc and one of your own. Legal moves are highlighted.

The game ends when neither player has a legal move. Whoever has more discs at that point wins.
